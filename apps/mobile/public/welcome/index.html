Built for the adventure

Less setup. More “let’s go.”

Play together. Stay in control.

Your location belongs to you.

Invite people you know. Review the rules together. Share only for the game you choose, and stop whenever you want.

  • Private by default
    No public player search. Joining a party does not enable GPS.
  • Only the view the game allows
    Secret objectives stay secret. Opponent pings can be delayed and approximate.
  • People before points
    Anyone can pause. No contact is required. Choose a safe venue and play on foot.

Open code. Your own playground.

Fieldplay is an independent OpenRecode application, licensed AGPL-3.0-only. No ads, subscriptions or mandatory proprietary game service.

Host on your own trusted infrastructure. X-Center is the project’s default hosting target. A trusted player host or dedicated server runs the rules; WebRTC may use a TURN relay when a direct connection is unavailable.
